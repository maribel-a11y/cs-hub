How to process refunds, exchanges, document requests, and chargebacks in Shopify and Richpanel

$
How to Process a Refund
Two paths: refund for a defective item (no return required) and refund after a return reaches the warehouse. Both are processed directly in Shopify.
Path A — Defective item (customer keeps it, last resort)
1
Confirm resolution sequence was followed
Before opening Shopify, verify in Richpanel that: (1) replacement was offered and declined, and (2) store credit was offered and declined. Refund is only processed after both options were rejected by the customer.
Never process a refund for a defective item without first confirming the customer declined replacement and store credit. This is the last option.
2
Open the order in Shopify
Search by order number or customer email. Open the order page. Verify the item being refunded matches what was reported as defective.
3
Click "Refund" — it's automatic
On the order page, click the Refund button. Select only the line item being refunded. Confirm the amount. Submit. The refund is processed automatically — no manual steps needed on the payment side.
Do not require the customer to return the defective item before processing this refund. They keep it.
4
Notify the customer with confirmation number
Reply in Richpanel confirming the refund was processed. Include: the refund confirmation number from Shopify, and the processing timeline: up to 15 calendar days depending on the bank, though most banks process it in 3–5 business days.
Always say "up to 15 calendar days" — this sets the right expectation. Most banks process faster, so the customer will be pleasantly surprised rather than disappointed.
5
Tag and close ticket in Richpanel
Tag: defective-product + refund-processed. Add internal note documenting the full resolution sequence (replacement offered → declined, store credit offered → declined, refund processed). Close ticket.
Path B — Return received at warehouse (standard return)
1
Wait for B3PL confirmation email
Do not process the refund until you receive a confirmation email from B3PL at 3pl@soap-bx.com confirming the item was received and inspected at the warehouse. This is non-negotiable.
Never process a refund before B3PL confirms receipt. The confirmation email is your trigger — not the tracking showing "delivered to warehouse".
2
Open the order in Shopify and click Refund
Search the order. Click Refund. Select the returned item(s). Confirm the amount matches the original item price. Submit. The process is fully automatic.
If the return is partial (31–45 days window): process store credit only, not cash. Manager approval required before processing.
3
Notify the customer
Reply in Richpanel confirming the refund was processed. Provide: the Shopify refund confirmation number, and the timeline — up to 15 calendar days, depending on their bank. Most process in 3–5 business days.
4
Tag and close ticket
Tag: return-completed + refund-processed. Add internal note with B3PL confirmation reference. Close ticket.

How to Process an Exchange
Exchange = return the original item + ship the new item. The new item only ships after B3PL confirms receipt of the return.
1
Initiate the return (same as standard return SOP)
Follow the Returns SOP Steps 1–6: verify eligibility in Shopify, send first reply with exchange confirmation, request label from B3PL, tag ticket in Richpanel. The exchange item does not ship until the return is received.
2
Confirm the exchange item details with the customer
Before the return ships, confirm: the exact item they want (name, size, color). Verify it is in stock in Shopify. If it is not in stock, inform the customer immediately and offer alternatives before they ship the return.
Do not accept a return for an exchange if the replacement item is out of stock — resolve the item selection first to avoid holding the customer's return with no resolution.
3
Wait for B3PL to confirm the return arrived
B3PL will email support@havnwear.com when the return is received and inspected. This email is your trigger to ship the exchange. Do not email B3PL to ship the new item before this confirmation arrives.
4
Email B3PL to ship the exchange item
Once B3PL confirms the return, email 3pl@soap-bx.com with subject: Exchange Ship — Order #[ORIGINAL ORDER NUMBER]. Include: customer full name, shipping address, exchange item (name, size, color), and reference to the original order number.
Subject line format: "Exchange Ship — Order #HV-XXXXXX" — using the original order number makes it easy to trace in both Shopify and B3PL's system.
5
Create Draft Order in Shopify at $0
In Shopify, create a new Draft Order for the exchange item. Set price to $0. Add a note referencing the original order number and "Exchange — return received [date]". This keeps inventory and records accurate.
6
Send tracking to the customer
Once B3PL provides the tracking number for the exchange shipment, reply to the customer in Richpanel: confirm the exchange is on its way, provide tracking number and estimated delivery.
7
Tag and close ticket
Tag: return-completed + exchange-shipped. Add internal note with original order, exchange item, B3PL confirmation date, and new tracking number. Close ticket.

Document Requests — Invoices, Order Confirmations, Duplicates
How to handle requests for order confirmations, duplicate invoices, purchase records, or any order documentation.
1
Identify what document the customer needs
Common requests: order confirmation email, invoice for expense reimbursement, proof of purchase, gift receipt, or duplicate of any previous communication. Ask clearly if the request is ambiguous.
2
Pull the order in Shopify
Search by order number or email. Verify the customer's identity matches the order before sending any documentation — never send order details to an unverified email address.
Security check: confirm the email the customer is contacting from matches the email on the order, or ask them to verify the order number and shipping address before sending documents.
3
Resend order confirmation from Shopify
In Shopify, open the order. Click "Resend order confirmation" — this sends the original confirmation email to the address on file. This covers most document requests automatically.
4
If a formal invoice is needed
For expense reimbursement or formal invoice requests: from the Shopify order page, use the Print option to generate a PDF of the order summary. This includes: order number, items, amounts, date, and shipping address. Send it as an attachment via email.
If the customer needs the invoice addressed to a company name or with a different billing detail: note this is not something Shopify allows retroactively. Escalate to manager if it becomes a billing/tax issue.
5
Confirm and close
Reply to the customer confirming the document was sent and to which email address. Tag ticket: document-request. Close ticket.

How to Handle a Chargeback
A chargeback is a bank-initiated dispute. Once filed, it takes legal precedence over any CS action. The agent's role is to stop, escalate, and document — not to resolve independently.
1
Identify the chargeback
A chargeback may appear as: a Shopify notification, a customer email mentioning "disputing with my bank", "filed a claim with my card", or "my bank is handling it". Any of these triggers the chargeback protocol immediately.
The moment a chargeback is identified — regardless of what stage the CS case is at — stop all pending actions: no refund, no replacement, no carrier claim, no label.
2
Tag and escalate immediately
In Richpanel: tag ticket chargeback-active. Add an internal note with: what the customer said, order number, current case status, and timestamp. Notify manager immediately — this ticket does not move without manager approval.
3
Reply to the customer (holding response)
Send the Chargeback Active template from the Response Templates section. Key points: acknowledge receipt, explain HAVN cannot take additional action while the dispute is open, and inform them they can contact their bank to withdraw it if they prefer to resolve directly with HAVN.
Do not offer any resolution in this message. Do not reference the specific order details, amounts, or internal case status. Keep it factual and neutral.
4
Manager handles the dispute response in Shopify
Shopify will notify HAVN of the chargeback via the Payments section. The manager is responsible for submitting the dispute response with evidence (order confirmation, tracking, delivery proof, communication history). The agent provides documentation support only.
Evidence to compile for the manager if requested: order confirmation, tracking number and delivery proof from carrier, all Richpanel communication screenshots, and any photos or evidence from the CS case.
5
Monitor for resolution
Bank chargeback processes typically take 30–90 days. Keep the ticket open with tag chargeback-active until the manager confirms the dispute is resolved. Do not close the ticket or contact the customer again without manager instruction.
6
Key rules — always apply
These rules are absolute and have no exceptions.
A chargeback and a carrier claim cannot run at the same time
Once a chargeback is filed, cancel any pending carrier claim immediately.
Do not reship or issue a refund while a chargeback is active
Any action taken would complicate the bank dispute and weaken HAVN's case.
Customer withdraws the chargeback and contacts HAVN directly
Confirm withdrawal with manager before resuming the CS case. Get confirmation in writing.
Customer filed chargeback but the order was delivered correctly
Compile all delivery evidence for the manager's dispute response. Do not contact the customer about the evidence.
